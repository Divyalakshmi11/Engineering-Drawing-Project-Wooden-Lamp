# Engineering-Drawing-Project-Wooden-Lamp
 A creatively designed wooden lamp that combines natural aesthetics, functional lighting, and sustainable materials. The project focuses on transforming wood into an elegant lamp structure while providing a warm and comfortable lighting experience. 

1. Introduction
The wooden lamp is a simple decorative lighting device made using a wooden body, LED 
light source, bulb holder and electrical wiring. The wooden body contains multiple drilled 
holes through which coloured light passes, producing an attractive ambient-lighting effect.

3. Objective
• To design and fabricate a compact wooden lamp.
• To provide attractive and soft illumination.
• To make use of natural and easily available materials.
• To develop a low-cost and energy-efficient lighting product.

4. Materials Used
• Wooden block
• LED/multicolour LED bulb
• Bulb holder
• Electrical wire
• Power plug/supply
• Drill and drill bits
• Sandpaper
• Wood adhesive/finishing material
LEDs are particularly suitable for wooden lamp projects because they produce considerably 
less heat than conventional incandescent lamps.

6. Working Principle
When the lamp is connected to the power supply, electrical energy is supplied to the LED. 
The LED produces light inside the wooden enclosure. The drilled holes allow the light to 
escape in different directions, creating illuminated spots and patterns on the surrounding 
surface.
Power Supply → Switch/Wire → LED Holder → LED → Light through Wooden Holes

8. Construction
1. A wooden block is selected and cut to the required size.
2. Holes are marked on the wooden surface according to the desired pattern.
3. The holes are drilled carefully.
4. The wooden surface is sanded and polished.
5. A hole is provided at the top for the bulb holder and wire.
6. The LED and holder are fitted.
7. The electrical connections are checked before powering the lamp.
8. The lamp is tested in a darker environment to observe the lighting effect.

6. Features
• Natural wooden appearance
• Decorative and aesthetic design
• Multicolour/ambient illumination
• Compact and portable
• Customizable hole patterns
• Low energy consumption when an LED is used
• Suitable for indoor decorative lighting

8. Applications 
• Bedroom/night lamp
• Study-room decoration
• Living-room ambient lighting
• Desk decoration
• Handmade gift product
• Interior decoration

9. Advantages
• Simple construction
• Attractive appearance
• Low power consumption
• Easy to customize
• Uses a renewable/natural material
• Provides both functional and decorative value

10. Safety
The electrical connections should be properly insulated, and the lamp should use an 
appropriate LED and holder. The wiring should be checked before operation. For mainspowered construction, electrical connections should be verified by a qualified person before use.

11. Conclusion
The wooden lamp successfully combines woodworking, basic electrical assembly and 
creative design. The drilled wooden body produces an attractive light pattern, while the LED 
provides an energy-efficient light source. The design can be further improved by adding a 
switch, dimmer, rechargeable battery or automatic lighting control
